How setup works ↓

Open it anywhere.

Your work laptop, a friend's computer, your phone. Sign in with a passkey and everything is there: Today, Upcoming, projects, checklists. It stays in sync with Things on your Mac, both ways, within a second or so.

Ask your AI. It knows your lists.

things3pp is an MCP server, so AI apps can read and change your Things. Where the app supports it, your list shows up right in the chat and you can tick things off there.

Works with Claude, Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and anything else that speaks MCP. From your Mac, or from Claude.ai and ChatGPT on the web. AI apps can add, schedule and complete to-dos. They can't delete anything.

Free. No catch.

No subscription and no account with us. It runs on your Mac and a free Cloudflare account that only you control, so your to-dos never touch our servers. We don't have any. The code is MIT-licensed.

  1. Put the web app and MCP server on your Cloudflare account.
    npx -y things3pp cloud deploy
  2. Link your Mac and create your passkey.
    npx -y things3pp connect
  3. Or skip the browser and just connect the AI apps on your Mac.
    npx -y things3pp mcp setup

Needs a Mac with Things 3 and Node.js 22. The cloud steps also need a free Cloudflare account and its cf CLI.
